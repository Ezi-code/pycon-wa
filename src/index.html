Weaving Ghana
into Python's global fabric.

PyCon WA is a community-built Python conference for Ghana and the wider West African region — three days of talks, workshops, and hallway conversations for the developers, students, and career-changers who are about to become the region's next generation of builders.

Why we're doing this

One cloth, three threads.

PyCon WA exists to make Python — and the doors it opens — reachable for people who haven't traditionally had a seat at the table. Everything on this site sits on three commitments.

Thread 01

Access

Low-cost and scholarship tickets, a beginner track that assumes nothing, and a venue chosen for how easy it is to reach — not how impressive it looks.

Thread 02

Mentorship

Structured pairing between first-time speakers and experienced ones, office hours with working engineers, and a CFP process that helps proposals get stronger, not just accepted or rejected.

Thread 03

Representation

A program committee and speaker lineup that actually looks like the region — women, students, self-taught developers, and people building outside of Accra's tech hubs.

Why Ghana, why now

A developer community that's ready to host, not just attend.

Ghana's tech scene has spent the last decade growing quietly — through university coding clubs, hackathons, bootcamps, and a steady stream of local meetups. What's been missing is a flagship event built around that community, rather than one that simply passes through it. PyCon WA is that event: designed in Accra, run by local and regional volunteers, and built to leave something behind after the closing keynote ends.

  • Reach

    Regional, not just national

    Open to attendees and speakers from across West Africa, with travel support prioritized for those coming from outside major cities.

  • Pipeline

    Built with universities

    Direct partnerships with computer science departments and student developer clubs to bring first-time conference-goers into the room.

  • After

    A community, not a one-off

    Workshop curricula, recorded talks, and organizer playbooks stay open-source after the event, for the next city that wants to run one.

Venue & getting there

Accra, and everyone it takes to reach it.

Venue

A central Accra venue, confirmed closer to the event and chosen for public transport access, step-free entry, and reliable connectivity. Full address and a map go here once booked.

Regional travel support

A limited travel fund helps cover transport for attendees and speakers coming from outside Accra, including from other West African countries.

Visa support

Official invitation letters are available on request for attendees and speakers who need one for a visa application — get in touch as early as possible.

Accessibility

Step-free access, a quiet room, and captioned livestreams for main-hall talks. Tell us what you need in your registration and we'll plan around it.
