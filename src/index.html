The tech map of Ghana has a gap.
We're closing it.

Every major Ghanaian tech gathering happens in Accra or Kumasi. The Upper West Region — home to over 800,000 people — has never hosted one. PyCon WA is the first, held in the region's capital, Wa: three days of talks and hands-on Python, completely free, built to put the north on the map for the companies, investors, and opportunities that keep passing it by.

The theme

Put Wa on the map.

PyCon WA is built around one idea: a region shouldn't have to move to the capital to reach the tech industry — the tech industry should be willing to come to it. Everything here — the name, the free entry, the location — is built around getting the Upper West Region its first real seat at Ghana's tech table.

About the name

"WA" is doing double duty. It's the standard shorthand for West Africa — but it's also, simply, the name of the city we're holding this in: Wa, capital of the Upper West Region. We didn't choose the acronym to fit the city. We chose the city because the acronym already fit.

Why Wa, why now

The north isn't missing a demand for tech. It's missing an invitation.

Ghana's tech ecosystem — hackathons, incubators, investor visits, developer meetups — is concentrated almost entirely in Accra and Kumasi. The Upper West Region sits at the far end of that map: under-visited, under-funded, and rarely mentioned when people talk about "Ghana's tech scene," despite a growing base of students and self-taught developers with no local event to bring them together. PyCon WA doesn't ask the region to travel south to be seen. It starts the conversation here.

    Sponsoring or speaking at PyCon WA is, for most tech companies, their first-ever presence in the Upper West Region — and the start of a relationship, not a one-off visit.

Registration

Free. On purpose.

This is the first tech conference the Upper West Region has ever hosted, and for a lot of attendees it'll be their first conference of any kind. A price tag — even a small one — is exactly the kind of barrier that keeps events like this from reaching the people who'd benefit most. So there isn't one.

✕   No entry fee, no hidden cost
  • Full access to both days, all talks and workshops
  • Lunch and refreshments included
  • Limited transport support for attendees from outside Wa
  • Priority given to students and first-time conference-goers

Venue & getting to Wa

All roads — and one runway — lead here.

Venue

A central venue in Wa, confirmed closer to the event and chosen for easy access from the main bus terminal and nearby schools. Full address and a map go here once booked.

Getting here

Wa is reachable by long-distance bus from Accra, Kumasi, and Tamale, and by domestic flight when scheduled. Exact routes and timetables will be posted here closer to the date.

Transport support

A limited fund covers transport for attendees traveling from districts across the Upper West Region and neighbouring areas — flag this when you register.

Accessibility

Step-free access and a quiet room on-site. Tell us what you need in your registration and we'll plan around it.
